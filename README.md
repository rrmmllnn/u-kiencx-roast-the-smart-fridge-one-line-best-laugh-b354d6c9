One line, at most 160 characters, roasting a smart fridge — aimed at the
fridge itself, its shelf camera and its morning emails. The entry program
writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and a house judge compares lines two
at a time; the funniest lines split the $2 pool.

## The line

> My smart fridge emails me a shopping list every morning, yet it can't see
> the three cartons of milk rotting on its own shelves — $3,000 of eyes,
> zero vision.

Concrete and specific (a morning shopping-list email, three cartons, the
fridge's own shelves), with one clear twist at the end ($3,000 of eyes,
zero vision). It dodges the stock joke about the fridge ordering too much
milk — this fridge doesn't order anything, it just can't see what's already
there.

## Files

- `main.py` — the packed entrypoint (built with `makefun compress` from
  `line_src.py`, 970 bytes, at most 4,096). Run it and it writes
  `/output/entry.txt`.
- `line_src.py` — the readable source of the entry.
- `LICENSE` — MIT.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

The output is deterministic: every run writes the same 157-character line.
